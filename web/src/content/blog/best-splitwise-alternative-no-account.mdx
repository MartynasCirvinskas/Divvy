## What actually made Splitwise good

Before we talk alternatives, it's worth understanding what made Splitwise compelling in the first place. The core was simple: you log who paid for what, the app figures out who owes whom, and you settle up. It handled unequal splits, multi-currency trips, and recurring costs. The math was right, and it synced across phones without any friction.

The friction came later, in the form of monetization. When the basics started disappearing behind a subscription, users started looking for something else.

## What to look for in a replacement

A good Splitwise alternative needs to handle the fundamentals without compromise:

- **No account barrier.** If your friend needs to sign up before they can see the shared expense, half your group will just Venmo you and forget it. The best tools use a group code or a shared link — you're in instantly.
- **Accurate math.** Integer-cent precision matters. Floating-point rounding errors lead to long-running tiny debts that never fully settle.
- **Debt minimization.** If A owes B, B owes C, and C owes A, a smart app collapses that into one or two payments. Fewer transactions means fewer awkward reminders.
- **Multi-currency support.** Anyone who's done a holiday with friends in another country knows the pain of juggling EUR and GBP in a spreadsheet.
- **No caps or timers.** Artificial limits on free features aren't features — they're frustrations.

## Settling debts the right way

One area where many Splitwise alternatives cut corners is the settle-up flow. Some apps make you record payments manually with no way to confirm the other side received them. Others show you a long list of individual debts without any attempt to simplify.

EvenJar handles this differently. When you're ready to settle, the app shows each person their own debts — what they owe and to whom — with a simple action to mark each one paid. You tap "I paid this back" on your row; the other person taps "They paid me back" on theirs. Balances update for everyone in the group immediately.

The debt minimization pass runs before this step, so you're never settling 12 individual transactions when 3 transfers would cover the same balances. In a group of five people with interleaved expenses, this can mean the difference between a 20-minute back-and-forth and two quick transfers.
