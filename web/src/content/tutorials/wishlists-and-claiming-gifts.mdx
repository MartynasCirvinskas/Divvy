EvenJar's wishlist feature is designed for group gifting: the birthday person adds what they'd like, friends claim items privately, and nobody accidentally buys the same thing twice — all without spoiling the surprise.

## Adding wish items

Anyone in the group can have a wishlist. To add items to yours:

1. Open your group and tap the **Wishlists** tab.
2. Tap **Add wish item**.
3. Enter a name for the item (e.g. "The Midnight Library — paperback") and an optional note with a link, size, or colour preference.
4. Tap **Save**. The item appears on your wishlist for all group members to see.

You can add as many items as you like and edit or remove them at any time.

## How claiming works (and why it's hidden from you)

When a friend wants to buy one of your wish items, they tap **Claim** on that item. Their claim is **visible to every group member except you** — the wishlist owner. This means:

- Your friends can coordinate ("I'm getting the book, can someone grab the candle?") without telling you.
- You never see who's getting you what, so the surprise is preserved.
- There's no risk of duplicates — once an item is claimed, everyone else sees it's taken.

## Claiming an item

As a group member viewing someone else's wishlist:

1. Browse the items on their wishlist.
2. Find something you'd like to give and tap **Claim**.
3. The item is now marked as claimed for you. Other members can see it's taken; the owner cannot.
4. If your plans change, tap **Unclaim** on the same item — no hard feelings.

## Preventing duplicate gifts

Because claims are visible to all members except the wishlist owner, the group naturally self-organizes:

- Before you claim something, you can see if it's already been taken.
- If you both want to chip in on a bigger item, coordinate via your usual messaging app — just don't mention it in the app where the owner might see notifications.

## Tips

- Add your wishlist items well before your birthday or occasion so friends have time to plan.
- Include direct links (to a shop page) in the item note so there's no guessing about which version you want.
