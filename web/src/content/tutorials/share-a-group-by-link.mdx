The fastest way to get everyone into your EvenJar group is to share the invite link. A friend taps it, confirms their display name, and they're in — no account or sign-up required.

## Generate the invite link

1. Open your group and tap **Share** (or the share icon in the group header).
2. EvenJar copies a link to your clipboard and opens your device's share sheet.
3. Send it via your messaging app of choice — WhatsApp, iMessage, Telegram, or any other.

## What the link looks like

EvenJar supports two link formats:

- **Web format:** `https://evenjar.app/g/CODE` — works in any browser and on any platform.
- **Deep-link format:** `evenjar://g/CODE` — opens the EvenJar app directly on devices that have it installed.

`CODE` is your group's unique 6-letter identifier. The characters are chosen from an unambiguous set (no O, 0, I, or 1) so the code is always easy to read and type.

## What the recipient sees

When your friend taps the link:

1. If EvenJar is installed, the app opens and shows a **Join group** confirmation screen with the group name.
2. They type a display name (visible only to group members).
3. They tap **Join** — done.

If EvenJar isn't installed yet, the link opens in the browser, prompts them to download the app, and then resumes the join flow automatically after install.

## Manual code entry as a fallback

If the link doesn't work (e.g. the message was forwarded in a way that broke the URL), anyone can join manually:

1. Open EvenJar and tap **Join group**.
2. Type the 6-letter code exactly as shown.
3. Confirm the name and tap **Join**.

The code is shown at the top of the group screen and in the share sheet, so you can always read it out or paste it as plain text.
